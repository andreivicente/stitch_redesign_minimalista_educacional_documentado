## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a premium, calm, and distraction-free learning ecosystem tailored for accessible multilingual education (Libras, Spanish, and English). It transitions away from high-density sales landers and repetitive badge overload into an architectural, sober digital space that emphasizes pedagogical clarity and effortless accessibility.

### Personality & Core Attributes
- **Architectural & Sober:** Ample white space, deliberate line work, structured typographic hierarchy, and purposeful content pacing replace noisy card clusters and decorative fluff.
- **Inclusive & Universally Readable:** High contrast ratios (WCAG AAA compliant for body copy), crisp letterforms, generous touch targets, and natural support for accessibility overlays, screen readers, and Libras video interfaces.
- **Refined & Contemporary:** Softened geometry with subtle micro-borders, deep royal indigo anchors, muted iris accents, and crisp slate neutral tones evoke academic rigor merged with modern EdTech refinement.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Editorial Modernism with Subtle Tonal Layering.** The system relies on precise typographic structure, crisp containment lines (ghost borders), progressive disclosure mechanisms (segment controls, light accordions, and cleanly separated persona views), and generous negative space to present complex learning modalities with calm authority.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined trio of deep indigo, subdued teal, and balanced slate neutrals. It deliberately minimizes bright visual clutter while maintaining strong semantic contrast for accessibility.

- **Primary (`#4C1D95`):** Deep Royal Indigo. Used for focal actions, dominant branding anchors, primary interactive buttons, and high-emphasis headings.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488`):** Muted Teal. Serves as a clear, grounding functional tone for secondary affirmations, mentor workflows, and balanced badges.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1`):** Refined Iris. Applied sparingly to interactive highlights, progressive indicator lines, and active segmented controls.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate Ink. The foundational color for typography and crisp structural framing, ensuring maximum legibility on light canvases.

### Color Governance & Ratios
1. **60% Foundation Canvas:** Natural clean surfaces (`#F8FAFC` canvas with `#FFFFFF` elevation tiles). Backgrounds avoid harsh pure whites across large spreads to prevent screen glare.
2. **30% Typographic Slate:** Dominant text hierarchy using `#0F172A` and `#64748B`.
3. **10% Brand Accents:** Focused application of primary indigo and teal. Badges and micro-tags must never exceed 10% of any viewport.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent editorial headlines with the clarity of **Inter** for long-form instructional copy and user interface controls.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Optical Hierarchy:** Never place bold subtitles under bold titles without a clear scale step (minimum 1.4x scale difference).
- **Line Length:** Paragraph blocks are strictly constrained to a maximum of `65ch` to maintain comfort for neurodivergent and low-vision learners.
- **Paragraph Spacing:** Body copy relies on margin-bottom defaults equal to `0.75em` rather than dual line breaks.
- **Label Tracking:** All uppercase and sub-heading micro-labels use positive tracking (`+0.03em`) for immediate legibility at small scale.

## Layout & Spacing

A restrained layout philosophy structured on an 8pt base grid ensures clarity, structural balance, and rhythmic breathing room.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column responsive fluid grid centered inside a `1200px` max-width container, using `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` section margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` side padding.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Dense side-by-side components automatically reflow into unified vertical stacks.

### Density & Breathing Room
Sections require significant vertical separation (`space-3xl` on desktop, `space-2xl` on mobile) to avoid overwhelming the learner. Modules within a section cluster around `space-md` to `space-xl` intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained primarily through tonal stratification and precise 1px boundary lines rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational canvas providing a soft, glare-free background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-subtle border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Shadow is optional and faint: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus States):** Card elevated slightly on user interaction with `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` and border transitioning to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Libras Windows):** Crisp surface with elevated shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)` accompanied by an accessible backdrop scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `4px` blur).

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometric rounding to convey approachability while maintaining a clean, structured silhouette.

### Corner Radius Tokens
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Standard Buttons):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers:** `1rem` (16px).
- **Segmented Switch Outer Containers:** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Badges, Filters, and Media Pills:** `9999px` (Full pill), applied sparingly to individual contextual indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#4C1D95` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height of 44px (minimum WCAG target), 16px horizontal padding. Hover state deepens to `#3B0764`. Focus produces a `2px offset` ring in `#7C3AED`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1px solid border in `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`. Hover reveals surface `#F8FAFC`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#4C1D95` or `#0F172A` text, used exclusively for secondary actions inside navigation and tables.

### Persona Segmented Switch (Aluno vs. Professor)
- A container with `#F1F5F9` background and `4px` internal padding.
- Interactive segment items toggle seamlessly with a sliding white pill base, active label colored `#0F172A` with subtle shadow (`0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`), and inactive label colored `#64748B`. Replaces redundant duplicate sections across the screen.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Eliminate repetitive checklists and decorative badge bursts. Use single-column micro-typography headers, clean metadata points separated by dividers, and direct primary action links.
- Course cards feature a unified visual aspect-ratio container for preview imagery/Libras interpreter previews, clean title, level tag, and duration indicator.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#4C1D95` with a `3px` soft glow ring (`rgba(76, 29, 149, 0.15)`).
- Error state: border shifts to `#E11D48` accompanied by descriptive assistive micro-copy below the input.

### Accordions & Progressive Disclosure
- Clean border-bottom rule (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- No bulky colored background cards for FAQs. A single expanding text toggle with minimalist arrow rotation keeps cognitive load minimal.

### Video / Accessibility Float (Libras Player)
- Floating frame anchored in the bottom corner with `12px` border radius, subtle dark stroke, and clear expand/collapse controls designed to never obscure primary educational text.
